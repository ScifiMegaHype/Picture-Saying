How To Play

Guess the phrase or saying shown in the picture.

The answer can be a phrase, saying, movie, song, celebrity name, ect; from hereon refered to as "saying".

  • The picture is a puzzle for a saying . The number of words is shown above the guess box.
  • Capital letters and punctuation don't matter.
  • The colour of each word changes after a guess to show how close you are, orange -> green.
  • The timer starts when you press Start Guessing and only stops when you get the saying right, you cannot quit. Reading this page while playing does not pause the timer.

Examples (if the saying is "Pink slip")

pinkbag
Pink is in the saying and in the correct spot. Bag is not in the saying.
slippink
Both words are in the saying but in the wrong spot.
pinkslippery
Slippery contains a word from the saying (slip), but it isn't the exact word. 3+ characters.
